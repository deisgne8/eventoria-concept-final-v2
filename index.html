EXPERTISE

Four disciplines.
One vision.

so nothing is lost between the idea and the event.

Give the moment a reason.

We begin with the story, the place and the audience, then shape a creative direction that answers what the occasion needs to say.

  • Audience and occasion insight
  • Story architecture and show narrative

Make the idea tangible.

  • Experience, spatial & scenic design
  • Art sculpture design and build

  • Show content, entertainment & costume
  • Artist, cast & performer management
  • Choreography, rehearsals & show direction

Carry it through.

Production and live operations turn a strong idea into a reliable night, with event operations, protocol and live delivery held in one view.

  • Production and live shows
  • Event operations and protocol
  • On-site delivery and show calling
